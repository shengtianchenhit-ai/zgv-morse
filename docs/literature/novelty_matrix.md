## Claim boundary imposed by the audit

The paper may claim a controlled connection, coefficient closure, and uniform
transition. It may not claim to have discovered anisotropic ZGV points, the
four-minimum/four-saddle pattern, ZGV beating, isotropic `t^-1/2` decay,
two-dimensional stationary phase, or the general Bessel identity.

The strongest currently supportable contribution sentence is:

> For the full Lamb-wave dispersion problem, we connect the established
> isotropic ZGV critical circle and the established eight anisotropic ZGV
> points through a coefficient-resolved weak-symmetry-breaking reduction, and
> derive and validate the associated uniform temporal crossover without fitted
> amplitude, phase, frequency, or time shift.
