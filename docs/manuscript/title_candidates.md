The first candidate is rejected because `universal` would require a title-level
qualification by the admissible source and angular-weight class, the joint
limit, and the phase-validity window.  The second is mathematically precise but
does not expose the temporal result and can be read as applying to anisotropic
elastic plates generally.  The third accurately states the mechanism and
consequence, but its unqualified first word is broader than the controlled
family.

## Selected title

**Weak Cubic Anisotropy Unfolds a Lamb-Wave ZGV Ring and Changes Its Local
Decay Law**

Every substantive term is supported: C1 fixes the ZGV ring, C2--C5 establish
the weak cubic coefficient closure and local unfolding, and C6--C7 establish
the change between the ring and isolated-point temporal regimes.  “Local”
prevents a claim about finite-boundary trapping or the complete dispersion
spectrum.  The title makes no priority claim and does not generalize the
controlled crossover beyond its registered source, branch, annulus, and
weak-anisotropy window.
